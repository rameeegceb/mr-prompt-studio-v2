# Business Requirements

# Purpose

This document defines the business requirements for **Mr. Prompt Studio**.

It explains the business problems the platform addresses, identifies stakeholders, defines business objectives, and establishes the functional capabilities required to achieve the product vision.

This document focuses on **business needs**, not technical implementation.

---

# Executive Summary

Artificial Intelligence has become an essential productivity tool across the enterprise.

Employees increasingly use AI assistants to create documents, analyze information, write code, generate content, summarize meetings, and solve business problems.

Although AI adoption continues to increase, organizations face a common challenge:

> Employees know how to use AI, but they do not consistently know how to communicate effectively with AI.

Prompt quality directly impacts AI output quality.

Without a standardized Prompt Engineering approach, organizations experience inconsistent AI results, duplicated effort, and fragmented knowledge.

Mr. Prompt Studio addresses this challenge by providing a centralized Prompt Engineering platform that enables employees to learn, create, improve, evaluate, and reuse prompts while preserving Prompt Engineering knowledge as an enterprise asset.

---

# Business Problem

The organization currently faces several challenges.

## Problem 1 — Inconsistent Prompt Quality

Employees create prompts based on personal experience.

There is no standard approach.

### Impact

- Poor AI responses
- Inconsistent quality
- Reduced productivity
- Repeated prompt rewriting

---

## Problem 2 — Knowledge Loss

Prompt knowledge remains inside individual AI conversations.

When conversations end, valuable knowledge is lost.

### Impact

- No organizational learning
- Repeated effort
- No reusable prompt assets

---

## Problem 3 — No Standardized Prompt Engineering

Different teams use different prompting techniques.

There is no agreed methodology.

### Impact

- Inconsistent AI outcomes
- Difficult onboarding
- Lack of governance

---

## Problem 4 — Limited Learning Resources

Employees have few structured learning opportunities for Prompt Engineering.

Learning is largely based on experimentation.

### Impact

- Slow adoption
- Variable skill levels
- Low confidence

---

## Problem 5 — Vendor Dependence

Prompting approaches often become tied to a specific AI platform.

### Impact

- Vendor lock-in
- Reduced flexibility
- Difficult migration to future AI platforms

---

# Business Opportunity

A centralized Prompt Engineering platform provides the opportunity to:

- Improve AI adoption.
- Increase employee productivity.
- Preserve organizational knowledge.
- Standardize Prompt Engineering practices.

# Business Constraints

The MVP shall not include:

- Authentication
- User management
- Team collaboration
- Approval workflows
- Analytics dashboards
- Enterprise governance
- Version control
- Database persistence
- AI provider billing
- Enterprise administration

These capabilities are reserved for future releases.

---

# Assumptions

The following assumptions apply:

- Employees have access to an approved AI provider.
- Prompt Engineering is an organizational capability.
- Existing Prompt Engineering content will be reused.
- Existing HTML learning content becomes the Learning Hub.
- Existing Prompt Studio logic becomes the Prompt Workbench.
- AI providers may change over time.
- Vendor independence is a strategic requirement.

# Business Requirements Summary

Mr. Prompt Studio addresses a strategic business need by transforming Prompt Engineering from an individual skill into an enterprise capability.

The platform enables employees to learn, create, improve, evaluate, and reuse prompts while preserving Prompt Engineering knowledge as a reusable organizational asset.

This business capability provides long-term value independent of any specific AI vendor and establishes a sustainable foundation for enterprise AI adoption.
