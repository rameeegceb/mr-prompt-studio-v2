# Future Roadmap

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the long-term evolution of **Mr. Prompt Studio** beyond the MVP.

It describes how the platform will mature from an Enterprise Prompt Engineering application into a comprehensive Enterprise Prompt Engineering & Knowledge Platform.

The roadmap aligns business strategy, product evolution, technical architecture, and enterprise AI adoption into a unified long-term vision.

---

# 2. Product Vision

Mr. Prompt Studio will become the enterprise's central platform for Prompt Engineering, AI literacy, organizational knowledge, and intelligent AI collaboration.

The platform will enable employees to:

- Learn Prompt Engineering
- Create and improve prompts
- Discover enterprise knowledge
- Reuse organizational assets
- Collaborate across teams
- Govern Prompt Engineering standards
- Work with multiple AI providers
- Continuously improve AI interactions

---

# 3. Strategic Vision

The platform evolves through five maturity stages.

```
Learning

↓

Prompt Engineering

↓

Knowledge Management

↓

```

Each stage builds on the capabilities delivered in the previous stage.

# 20. Future Roadmap Summary

Mr. Prompt Studio is designed to evolve far beyond a Prompt Engineering tool.

Its long-term vision is to become the enterprise's authoritative platform for Prompt Engineering, AI literacy, reusable knowledge, and intelligent collaboration.

By expanding through carefully planned phases—Personal Productivity, Enterprise AI, Knowledge Management, Governance, and Enterprise Intelligence—the platform will enable organizations to build lasting Prompt Engineering capabilities while remaining independent of any specific AI provider.

This roadmap provides a strategic foundation for sustainable product evolution and positions Mr. Prompt Studio as a core capability within the enterprise AI ecosystem.
