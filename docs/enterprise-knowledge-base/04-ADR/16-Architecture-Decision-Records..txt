# Architecture Decision Records (ADR)

---

# Purpose

This document captures the key architectural decisions made during the design and evolution of **Mr. Prompt Studio**.

Each Architecture Decision Record (ADR) explains:

- The problem being solved
- The decision made
- The rationale
- Alternatives considered
- Consequences
- Future considerations

This document serves as the architectural memory of the project and should be updated whenever significant architectural decisions are made.

---

# ADR-001 — Build an Enterprise Platform, Not a Chatbot

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

The original implementation began as a Custom GPT focused on Prompt Engineering.

As enterprise demand increased, it became clear that users required a structured software platform rather than another conversational AI assistant.

---

## Decision

Mr. Prompt Studio will be designed as an **Enterprise Prompt Engineering & Knowledge Platform**.

It will not be positioned as:

- A chatbot
- An AI assistant
- A replacement for Microsoft Copilot
- A replacement for ChatGPT

---

## Rationale

Enterprise value comes from enabling Prompt Engineering capabilities rather than replacing existing AI assistants.

---

## Consequences

Positive

- Clear product identity
- Long-term product strategy
- Enterprise scalability

Negative

- Requires broader application architecture
- Larger implementation scope

---

# ADR-002 — Knowledge First Architecture

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

Prompt Engineering knowledge is often lost inside AI conversations.

Organizations need reusable knowledge rather than temporary conversations.

---

## Decision

Knowledge becomes a first-class architectural layer.

Enterprise knowledge includes:

- Learning content
- Prompt templates
- Frameworks
- Best practices
- Evaluation rules
- Scoring models

---

## Rationale

Knowledge represents long-term organizational intellectual property.

---

## Consequences

Benefits

- Reuse
- Consistency
- Vendor independence

---

# ADR-003 — AI Provider Independence

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

AI providers continue to evolve rapidly.

The application should not depend on a single provider.

---

## Decision

Introduce an AI Provider abstraction layer.

All AI communication passes through:

```
Feature

↓

AI Service

↓

Provider Factory

↓

Provider
```

---

## Rationale

Avoid vendor lock-in while simplifying future integrations.

---

## Consequences

Benefits

- Provider flexibility
- Easier testing
- Long-term maintainability

Trade-off

- Additional abstraction layer

---

# ADR-004 — Feature-Based Modular Architecture

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

The application contains multiple independent business capabilities.

---

## Decision

Organize the codebase by feature rather than by technical layer alone.

Each feature owns:

- Components
- Pages
- Services
- Hooks
- Context
- Models
- Utilities

---

## Rationale

Improves maintainability, ownership, and scalability.

---

## Consequences

Benefits

- Reduced coupling
- Better organization
- Independent evolution

---

# ADR-005 — Business Logic Outside UI

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

Business logic embedded within UI components becomes difficult to maintain and test.

---

## Decision

Business logic resides in services and engines.

UI components focus exclusively on presentation and user interaction.

---

## Rationale

Supports separation of concerns and improves testability.

---

## Consequences

Benefits

- Cleaner components
- Better unit testing
- Reusable services

---

# ADR-006 — Prompt Workbench as the Core Product Experience

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

Prompt Engineering involves multiple related activities.

Creating separate tools for each activity increases complexity.

---

## Decision

Consolidate Prompt Engineering capabilities into a unified **Prompt Workbench**.

Capabilities include:

- Create
- Improve
- Evaluate
- Compare
- Convert
- Score
- Framework Recommendation

---

## Rationale

Users should complete Prompt Engineering tasks within a single workspace.

---

## Consequences

Benefits

- Simplified navigation
- Better user experience
- Unified workflow

---

# ADR-007 — Reuse Existing Enterprise Assets

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

Several valuable assets already exist.

Examples include:

- Prompt Engineering HTML Guide
- Prompt Studio GPT logic
- Microsoft Copilot Agent
- Enterprise prompt templates

---

## Decision

Reuse these assets rather than recreating them.

---

## Rationale

Accelerates delivery while preserving proven enterprise knowledge.

---

## Consequences

Benefits

- Reduced implementation effort
- Faster MVP delivery
- Consistent Prompt Engineering guidance

---

# ADR-008 — Layered Architecture

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

A clear separation between presentation, business logic, knowledge, and AI execution improves maintainability.

---

## Decision

Adopt a layered architecture.

```
Presentation

↓

Application

↓

Knowledge

↓

Prompt Engineering

↓

AI Integration

↓

AI Providers
```

---

## Rationale

Improves scalability and simplifies future enhancements.

---

## Consequences

Benefits

- Clear responsibilities
- Easier testing
- Lower coupling

---

# ADR-009 — Context Builder Before AI Execution

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

Sending raw user prompts directly to AI often produces inconsistent results.

---

## Decision

Introduce a Context Builder that enriches requests with structured enterprise knowledge before AI execution.

---

## Rationale

Structured context improves response quality and consistency.

---

## Consequences

Benefits

- Better AI responses
- Reusable context model
- Explainable processing

---

# ADR-010 — Repository Pattern for Enterprise Knowledge

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

Learning content, templates, and Prompt Engineering assets should be managed independently of storage implementation.

---

## Decision

Use repositories to abstract knowledge access.

Examples:

- CourseRepository
- TemplateRepository
- KnowledgeRepository

---

## Rationale

Allows future migration from static files to databases or APIs without affecting business logic.

---

## Consequences

Benefits

- Storage independence
- Easier testing
- Flexible evolution

---

# ADR-011 — React Context for Shared State

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

The MVP does not require a complex state management framework.

---

## Decision

Use React Context with custom hooks for shared feature state.

---

## Rationale

Provides sufficient capability while minimizing complexity.

---

## Consequences

Benefits

- Simpler architecture
- Reduced dependencies
- Easy maintenance

Future Consideration

Evaluate advanced state management only if application complexity justifies it.

---

# ADR-012 — Prompt Engineering as a Business Capability

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

Prompt Engineering is often treated as an AI feature.

---

## Decision

Treat Prompt Engineering as a standalone business capability.

AI supports Prompt Engineering rather than defining it.

---

## Rationale

Keeps the product focused on enterprise capability building.

---

## Consequences

Benefits

- Strong product differentiation
- Long-term strategic value
- Vendor independence

# ADR-014 — AI Enhances, Application Owns

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

AI models excel at language generation but should not control application workflows.

---

## Decision

The application owns:

- Navigation
- Business workflows
- Prompt Engineering methodology
- Knowledge
- Validation

AI providers perform language generation only.

---

## Rationale

Maintains predictable behavior and simplifies governance.

---

## Consequences

Benefits

- Explainable architecture
- Consistent business rules
- Easier provider replacement

---

# ADR-016 — Documentation as an Architectural Asset

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

Traditional documentation quickly becomes outdated and often describes files rather than explaining the system.

---

## Decision

Treat architecture documentation as a living knowledge asset.

Documentation should explain:

- Why the system exists
- How business capabilities interact
- Architectural decisions
- Product evolution
- Future extension points

rather than simply listing files or classes.

---

## Rationale

Improves onboarding, architectural consistency, and long-term maintainability.

---

## Consequences

Benefits

- Better knowledge transfer
- Faster onboarding
- Stronger architectural governance

# ADR Summary

These Architecture Decision Records establish the foundational principles that guide the evolution of Mr. Prompt Studio.

They ensure the platform remains:

- Knowledge-first
- Vendor-independent
- Modular
- Scalable
- Enterprise-focused
- Maintainable
- Extensible

Future architectural decisions should build upon these principles and be documented as additional ADRs to preserve the project's architectural history and support informed decision-making throughout the platform's lifecycle.
